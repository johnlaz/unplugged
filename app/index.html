About
Unplugged
Instrument collection, tuner, chords and practice tools
v2.6
Your data stays with you. Your collection, notes and settings are stored on this device. The app talks to the internet only for AI features (Groq, using your own key), instrument photos (Wikipedia), lyrics lookup, and the spreadsheet library. Backups never include your API key unless you choose to.

Backup & restore

Your collection lives on this device. A backup is one file you can keep in cloud storage, email to yourself, or use to move to a new phone.

Back up

Saves everything in a single .json file: instruments, photos, gig and service logs, notes, setlists and your own songs.

Restore

Choose a backup file. Merge adds new instruments and updates matching ones. Replace all swaps everything for the backup.
